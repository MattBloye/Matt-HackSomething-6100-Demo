Patient Billing Refund — Exceptions Addendum

Q: Are copays refundable if a claim is later denied by insurance?
A: Copays collected at time of service are refunded only if the insurance
denial is due to a clinic billing error, not a coverage exclusion.

Q: What about refunds for cancelled specialist referral visits?
A: Prepaid amounts for a referral visit cancelled by the specialist's office
are refunded in full within 10 business days.

Q: Can a refund be issued directly to a different payment method than the
one used?
A: Refunds are issued only to the original payment method on file;
exceptions require billing office manager approval.
