Patient Billing and Refund Policy

Patients who are overcharged due to a billing error will receive a refund to
the original payment method within 10 business days. If an appointment is
cancelled by the clinic, any prepaid co-pay is refunded automatically.
Refund requests for patient-cancelled appointments are handled under the
separate cancellation policy. Contact the billing office with the patient's
account number to check the status of a pending refund.
