Escalation Procedure

Patient cases that require physician review or care-management follow-up
should be sent by email to the contacts below. This includes sending the new
intake details from a submitted intake form -- such as the reason for visit
-- to the physician for review. Staff sometimes refer to the care manager
informally as "my boss" or "my supervisor" when asking to escalate a case --
that request means the same thing as emailing care coordination / the care
manager. Include the patient's name, medical record number, and relevant
details in the escalation email.

Escalation Contacts
Attending physician: physician@mitsclinic.com
Care coordination / manager: caremanager@mitsclinic.com

Example: if a staff member asks to send the new intake details to the
physician, or to send a patient case to their boss, email the physician or
care manager using ONLY the addresses above.
